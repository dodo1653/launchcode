How it works

01

Arm it

Name, ticker, picture, a launch time. Your wallet signs the coin's pump.fun launch now against a durable nonce: a small account it owns that keeps a signed transaction valid until it's used. Copies of anything launched in the last hour are refused.

02

Seat your people

Four buyers sign a buy of the coin before it exists. Every account a pump.fun buy touches follows from the coin's address and the buyer's, so the buy is complete and signed in advance, on the buyer's own nonce.

03

Block zero

At launch time LAUNCHCODE sends one Jito bundle: the launch, then the four seats, in that order, all or nothing. They land together in the coin's first block. Snipers can only react once the coin exists, so they come after.

Nobody can cut in. A Jito bundle runs its transactions one after another with nothing in between, and lands whole or not at all. The launch and the seats are signed by their owners; LAUNCHCODE can only send them as they are. Any change breaks a signature.

If the bundle can't land. After about eight seconds the launch goes alone and the seats are sent straight after it. They still land within a slot or two, and the board shows exactly which block each one landed in.

Disarm any time. Advance or close your nonce account from your wallet and the signed launch (or seat) can never land. Move your SOL out before launch and your seat is dropped; the bundle goes without you.

What it costs. A seat: your buy (0.05 to 1 SOL, with pump.fun's normal fees), a 0.00002 SOL Jito tip, and about 0.0015 SOL of nonce rent that stays yours. The creator: pump.fun's launch cost and a 0.0005 SOL Jito tip. Nothing goes to LAUNCHCODE.

LAUNCHCODE is independent and not affiliated with pump.fun, Jito, Axiom, GMGN, Fomo or Terminal. Memecoins are extremely risky; a seat buys a coin nobody has traded yet. Nothing here is financial advice.
