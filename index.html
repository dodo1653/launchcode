How it works

01

Sign it now

Name, ticker, picture. Your wallet signs the coin's pump.fun launch against a durable nonce: a small account it owns that keeps a signed transaction valid until it's used.

02

Pick a trigger

Words in a headline from seven newsrooms (plus Google News for your words), a wallet's next transaction, SOL crossing a price, a pump.fun coin graduating or reaching a market cap, or a time. Armed for up to 72 hours.

03

It fires

The relay checks every few seconds. When the trigger hits, it sends your signed launch to the network and to Jito at once. You're live while everyone else is still typing the ticker.

Sealed until it fires. The board shows the trigger and the dev wallet, never the coin. Nobody can read your ticker off the page and launch it first. The relay itself refuses copies of anything launched in the last hour.

Nothing can change it. The launch is signed by your wallet before it's armed. The relay can only send it as it is; any change breaks your signature. If you're the dev, you're the dev, asleep or not.

Disarm any time. Advance or close your nonce account from your wallet and the signed launch can never land. Unfired codes expire after the hours you picked.

What it costs. About 0.0015 SOL of rent for your nonce account (yours to reclaim), pump.fun's normal launch cost when it fires, and a 0.0005 SOL tip to Jito. Nothing goes to LAUNCHCODE.

LAUNCHCODE is independent and not affiliated with pump.fun, Jito or any newsroom it reads. Memecoins are extremely risky. Nothing here is financial advice.
